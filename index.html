Privacy policy

Don’t Break the Pattern

Effective date: October 22, 2026
App: Don’t Break the Pattern (Android package com.ninjanext.dontbreakthepattern)
Developer: NinjaNext (Muhammad Saqib Javed) — ninjanexttech.com
Contact: info@ninjanexttech.com

Don’t Break the Pattern is an offline brain-puzzle game. It needs no account, shows no ads, contains no analytics and doesn’t connect to the internet while you play. This policy explains the little the game stores on your device and confirms that NinjaNext collects none of it.

No data collected

Nothing about you or how you play is sent to NinjaNext or anyone else.

Stays on your device

Scores, coins, themes and settings are saved locally.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. No accounts.

Vibration only

The only device feature used is vibration, for optional haptics.

Information the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, Don’t Break the Pattern stores the following only on your device, in local storage inside the app’s private storage:

  • Your best score and simple statistics (games played, correct answers, best combo, highest difficulty reached, puzzle types solved, fast answers)
  • Your coin balance, the colour themes you’ve unlocked and the theme you’re using
  • Daily challenge results (your best score for each day you played and your daily streak)
  • Achievements you’ve unlocked and when
  • Settings: sound effects, haptics (vibration) and reduced motion

None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device. Puzzles, including the daily challenge, are generated on your device.

Information we collect

NinjaNext doesn’t collect, receive, sell or share any personal or usage information. Don’t Break the Pattern has no user accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. NinjaNext can’t see your scores or progress.

Sound effects

The game plays short sound effects that are bundled inside the app. It never records audio and doesn’t use the microphone. You can turn sound effects off in the game’s settings.

Permissions

The game doesn’t request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or network state. It uses vibration for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game’s settings.

Links that leave the game

The game never goes online by itself. It only opens something outside the app when you tap a link:

  • “More from NinjaNext”, in the Home screen’s settings sheet and on the game-over summary, opens the Google Play page of another NinjaNext app in the Google Play app (or your browser).
  • The privacy policy link opens this page in your browser.

Once you leave the game, Google Play and your browser are governed by their own privacy policies. Don’t Break the Pattern doesn’t send anything to them about you or your games.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Clear the app’s storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Don’t Break the Pattern → Storage → Clear storage)
  • Uninstall the game, which removes its stored data

Because your progress exists only on your device, deleting it is permanent unless your device’s own backup includes it. NinjaNext can’t recover it for you.

Children’s privacy

Don’t Break the Pattern contains no violence, chat, purchases or ads, and is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children, including children under 13. The only links out of the game (described above) open only when a player taps them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a feature is added, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any feature that would send data off your device would be optional, off by default, and would ask for your consent first.
